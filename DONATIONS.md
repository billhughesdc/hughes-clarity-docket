## Why three networks

The Clarity Act concerned **market structure for digital assets as they are actually held**. Retail in the United States holds bitcoin, EVM-network assets, and solana-ecosystem assets. This docket therefore accepts support in those same rails. Choosing one chain would have been a branding exercise.
