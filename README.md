### Why this exists

On **15 September 2026**, the United States Senate voted **49–50** on a motion to proceed to the **Digital Asset Market Clarity Act**. Sixty votes were required. The motion failed. Four Republicans joined a unified Democratic caucus in opposition. Principal public reporting places the breakdown on ethics language concerning executive-branch and family financial interests in digital assets, after more than a year of negotiation.

Market participants, counsel, and retail holders are left where they were: **no enacted market-structure statute**, continued dual-agency uncertainty (SEC / CFTC), and a congressional calendar compressed by midterm elections.

This docket asks Congress not to treat the failed motion as the end of the file.
